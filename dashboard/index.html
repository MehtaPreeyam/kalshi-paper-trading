How the bot decides
  • From 2:30 left in each 15-minute market it rechecks on every price change, and buys the favourite the first moment every check passes. At most one trade per market.
  • Until 2:00 left the bar is higher: the coins need a 1.3× bigger base buffer (like BTC), gold and silver a 1.2× bigger volatility buffer.
  • Base buffer: a set percent of the price at the start of the window. 1.5× bigger in the 9:30–10:30 AM stock-market open.
  • Volatility buffer: a multiple of the coin's normal 1-minute move, scaled to the time left. It shrinks as the close gets closer.
  • Needed: the bigger of the two. The coin has to be at least that far past the line.
  • Kalshi's price also has to be 95–99.5¢, and the coin can't be moving sharply against the bet.
  • For a skipped market, the numbers are from the moment it came closest to buying.
  • BTC uses dollar buffers instead of percents: $85 base ($125 at the open), 1.3× bigger from 2:30 to 2:00 left, and a 2.5× volatility buffer. It also skips a market when BTC jumps against the bet or most of the last 30 seconds of Kalshi money goes the other way.
  • Prices: Coinbase for the coins. Hyperliquid for gold and silver, measured from Hyperliquid's own price at the window start.

$100 a trade, bought off Kalshi's live order book (real asks and sizes, up to 99.5¢), fee taken out. No order is sent, so a fill is assumed where the live bots sometimes miss one.
